Notes:
  • The ADS1115 and the LCD are on two separate I2C buses; do not join their SDA/SCL wires.
  • The DFRobot Gravity Dissolved Oxygen sensor does not connect directly to the ESP32. Its analog output goes to ADS1115 channel A0; the ADS1115 talks to the ESP32 over GPIO 21/22.
  • GPIO 16/17 are taken by the MAX3485 wires even when RS485 is disabled in firmware.
  • GPIO 4 (DRV8833 Sleep/STBY) is not used: the driver board's sleep pin is tied on internally (MOTOR_STBY_CONNECTED = 0).
  • All modules share GND with the ESP32, including the SEN0711's separate 10–30 V supply.
Changes since rev. 2: ADS1115 and LCD split onto separate buses (ADS1115 SDA GPIO 18 → 21; LCD on GPIO 25/27) • RS485 RX GPIO 4 → 16 • RS485 module replaced by a MAX3485 with DE/RE on GPIO 32 • Fan IN1 GPIO 25 → 18, IN2 GPIO 13 → 2 • DRV8833 Sleep pin no longer wired (was GPIO 27) • ADS1115 address 0x48 → 0x49 • BOOT button added for Wi-Fi setup.
